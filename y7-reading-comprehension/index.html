Year 7 Reading Comprehension Practice

Seven short texts. Read each one carefully, answer the questions, then check your work.

    How this works
    • Each text is scored out of 4: a retrieval multiple-choice question, a retrieval short answer, a vocabulary question and an inference question. These are marked when you press Check answer.
    • Each multiple-choice question can be checked once. A short answer gets one more try if the first answer is wrong. Give one answer, not a list of guesses.
    • The how do you know? and Point-Evidence-Explain answers are not marked. Write your answer first, then compare it with the model answer.
    • Your score for each text is shown at the top of the text. Use Try this text again to start a text over.
    • Saving is off unless you tick Save my progress. Then answers are kept only in this browser on this device. On a shared computer, leave it off. Nothing is sent anywhere and there is no sign-in.
    All texts and questions are original, written for Year 7 (KS3) practice. No sign-in, no tracking, nothing leaves your device.
